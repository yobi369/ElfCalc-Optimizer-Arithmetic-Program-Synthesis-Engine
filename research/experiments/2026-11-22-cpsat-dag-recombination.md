# CP-SAT DAG recombination pilot — 22 November 2026

## Baseline

The frozen candidate is the 131,052-instruction per-puzzle merge of the Juan-Severiano 132,515 checkpoint and Ishan Karnick frontier dataset v18. It has 1,000/1,000 valid and complete programs.

## Experiment

The GoodPJW v151 `recombine.py` CP-SAT implementation was run on the 20 puzzles with the largest gap between program length and the elementary target-count lower bound. The optimizer received recipes from the 131,052 baseline and the attributed Juan, Ishan, and Asterios v297 candidate banks.

Two variants were tested:

1. **Existing recipe pool:** no cross-recipe or bridge-recipe expansion; CP-SAT was allowed to choose a minimum value DAG from existing candidate recipes.
2. **Expanded recipe pool:** cross-recipe and bridge-recipe expansion enabled, with an 8-second CP-SAT limit per puzzle; a second pass constrained ordering using the baseline program.

## Results

- No strict, register-valid improvement was produced in either variant.
- In the existing-recipe variant, CP-SAT returned an optimal objective equal to the current instruction count for all 20 puzzles.
- In the expanded variant, the solver returned feasible/optimal objectives equal to the current instruction count for all 20 puzzles.
- The upstream greedy DAG-to-register scheduler returned `None` for every selected DAG. Consequently, no candidate could be serialized as a valid 48-register program.
- The full-submission audit retained the 131,052 baseline unchanged.

The result is not evidence that no shorter arithmetic DAG exists. The CP-SAT objective minimizes the number of unique values but does not jointly enforce a feasible 48-register schedule. A one-node-per-value DAG can also have longer live ranges than a program that recomputes an intermediate. This is the likely structural bottleneck to address next, but it remains a research hypothesis rather than a proven lower bound.

## Next technical direction

1. Add register-pressure constraints to DAG selection, or permit controlled recomputation/rematerialization of values.
2. Improve topological scheduling and register allocation, with explicit peak-live-register measurement.
3. Keep the exact interpreter as the final authority: only candidates that replay successfully and cover every target may replace the baseline.

## Artifacts

The experiment outputs were generated by GitHub Actions and include the target-puzzle list, per-puzzle CP-SAT logs, selected DAG JSONL records, unchanged submission, and replay report. The GoodPJW source attribution and license qualification are documented in [goodpjw-v151-recombine-ATTRIBUTION.md](../upstream/goodpjw-v151-recombine-ATTRIBUTION.md).
